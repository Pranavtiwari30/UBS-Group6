INTERNATIONAL SANCTIONS RULES — UBS PAYMENTS COMPLIANCE
Version: 3.1 | Effective: January 2024 | Classification: Internal Use Only

=======================================================================
1. SANCTIONS FRAMEWORKS APPLICABLE
=======================================================================

UBS complies with all of the following sanctions regimes:

  - OFAC (Office of Foreign Assets Control) — United States
  - HM Treasury — United Kingdom
  - EU Consolidated Sanctions List — European Union
  - UN Security Council Consolidated List
  - SECO (State Secretariat for Economic Affairs) — Switzerland
  - MAS Sanctions List — Singapore

Any conflict between regimes is resolved by applying the most
restrictive applicable requirement.

=======================================================================

=======================================================================

The following countries are subject to COMPREHENSIVE sanctions.
ALL transactions to/from these jurisdictions are PROHIBITED
regardless of amount, purpose, or beneficiary.

  - Iran (IR / IRN)

5. SANCTIONS SCREENING PROCEDURE
=======================================================================

5.1 SCREENING TRIGGERS
    Every outgoing and incoming payment must be screened against:
    a) Beneficiary full name (fuzzy match, >80% similarity threshold)
    b) Beneficiary bank name and BIC/SWIFT code
    c) Beneficiary country of residence and account country
    d) Originator name (for incoming payments)
    e) Payment reference text (free-text fields)

5.2 MATCH CATEGORIES
    - TRUE POSITIVE: Confirmed sanctions match → BLOCK immediately
    - FALSE POSITIVE: No actual match after investigation → RELEASE
    - POTENTIAL MATCH: Requires human review within 4 business hours

5.3 BLOCKING PROCEDURE
    - Payment must be held in suspense account
    - Funds must NOT be credited to beneficiary
    - Compliance must be notified within 30 minutes of detection
    - Regulatory report filed within 24 hours (OFAC requires within 10 days)

5.4 PROHIBITED EVASION TECHNIQUES
    The following activities are red flags for sanctions evasion:
    - Substituting sanctioned country codes with neighbouring countries
    - Using front companies or intermediaries in non-sanctioned countries
    - Breaking large payments into smaller amounts to avoid screening
    - Using personal accounts to transact on behalf of sanctioned entities
    - Providing false payment purpose codes

=======================================================================
6. SWIFT GPI AND SANCTIONS
=======================================================================

- SWIFT gpi payments carry a unique end-to-end transaction reference (UETR)
- Any SWIFT payment to a sanctioned SWIFT BIC must be rejected at the
  gateway before transmission
- Correspondent bank instructions (field 57) must also be screened
- Do not use "cover payments" that obscure the original parties

=======================================================================
7. PENALTIES FOR NON-COMPLIANCE
=======================================================================

Sanctions violations carry severe penalties:

  - Civil penalties: Up to USD 1 million per violation (OFAC)
  - Criminal penalties: Up to 20 years imprisonment per violation
  - Reputational damage and potential loss of US dollar clearing access
  - Swiss FINMA enforcement action and banking licence risk

All staff are personally liable for willful sanctions violations.
"I didn't know" is not a defence if proper screening was bypassed.
