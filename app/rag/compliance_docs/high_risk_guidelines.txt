HIGH-RISK GEOGRAPHY & TRANSACTION GUIDELINES — UBS PAYMENTS
Version: 2.8 | Effective: January 2024 | Classification: Internal Use Only

=======================================================================
1. GEOGRAPHIC RISK CLASSIFICATION FRAMEWORK
=======================================================================

UBS classifies all countries into four risk tiers for payment processing.
Risk tier determines the level of due diligence and approval required.

TIER 1 — STANDARD RISK (Normal processing)
  - G7 nations: US, UK, Germany, France, Italy, Japan, Canada
  - EU/EEA member states (excluding high-risk flagged members)
  - Singapore, Hong Kong SAR, Australia, New Zealand, Switzerland
  - South Korea, Japan, Israel, UAE (with enhanced monitoring)

TIER 2 — ELEVATED RISK (Enhanced Due Diligence required)
  - India, Brazil, Mexico, South Africa, Turkey, Indonesia
  - Most of Eastern Europe (excluding EU members)
  - Middle East (excluding Gulf states with clean FATF ratings)
  - China (PRC) — heightened capital controls monitoring

TIER 3 — HIGH RISK (Senior Compliance Officer approval required)
  - Countries on FATF Grey List (monitored jurisdictions):
    * Bulgaria, Burkina Faso, Cameroon, Croatia, Democratic Republic
      of Congo, Haiti, Jamaica, Mali, Mozambique, Nigeria, Philippines,
      Senegal, South Africa, South Sudan, Syria, Tanzania, Vietnam
  - Offshore Financial Centres (OFCs) with weak AML regimes:
    * British Virgin Islands (BVI), Cayman Islands, Vanuatu,
      Seychelles, Marshall Islands, Samoa

TIER 4 — PROHIBITED (Comprehensive sanctions — BLOCK all transactions)
  - Iran, North Korea, Syria, Cuba
  - Crimea, Donetsk, Luhansk regions (treated as separate from Ukraine)
  - See sanctions_rules.txt for full list and procedures

=======================================================================
2. HIGH-RISK AMOUNT THRESHOLDS BY GEOGRAPHY
=======================================================================

Transactions to Tier 2 countries:
  - Above USD 5,000: Enhanced beneficiary verification required
  - Above USD 25,000: Supporting documentation mandatory
  - Above USD 100,000: Relationship Manager sign-off required

Transactions to Tier 3 countries:
  - Above USD 1,000: AML analyst review required
  - Above USD 10,000: Senior AML Officer approval mandatory
  - Above USD 50,000: Automatic escalation to CCO level

Transactions to Tier 4 (sanctioned) countries:
  - ANY amount: BLOCK and FILE REPORT

=======================================================================
3. HIGH-RISK BUSINESS SECTORS
=======================================================================

The following business types require enhanced scrutiny regardless
of geography:

  HIGHEST RISK:
    - Cryptocurrency exchanges and virtual asset service providers (VASPs)
    - Money service businesses (MSBs) and hawala operators
    - Cash-intensive businesses (casinos, pawn shops, car dealers)
    - Arms and defence contractors (without government contracts)
    - Precious metals and gemstone dealers

  HIGH RISK:
    - Real estate developers and agents (property-based laundering)
    - Import/export trading companies with opaque supply chains
    - Luxury goods retailers (art, yachts, private jets)
    - Private banking clients with >USD 1M assets
    - Non-profit organisations (NPOs) receiving foreign funds

  MODERATE RISK:
    - Law firms and accountancy firms (legal privilege risk)
    - Trust and company service providers (TCSPs)
    - Hedge funds and private equity with complex ownership

=======================================================================
4. INDICATORS OF SUSPICIOUS GEOGRAPHIC PATTERNS
=======================================================================

4.1 LAYERING RED FLAGS
    - Payment routed through 3 or more countries before final destination
    - Round-trip transactions (funds sent out and returned within 30 days)
    - Use of correspondent banks in high-risk jurisdictions as intermediaries
    - Multiple currency conversions with no clear business justification

4.2 ROUTING ANOMALIES
    - SWIFT field 56 (Intermediary Bank) in a Tier 3 country when
      beneficiary is in a Tier 1 country
    - Payment purpose code inconsistent with the geographic corridor
    - Sudden change in beneficiary country compared to historical pattern

4.3 TRADE-BASED MONEY LAUNDERING (TBML)
    - Invoice amounts significantly above or below market value
    - Multiple payments for the same invoice number
    - Goods description inconsistent with payment corridor
      (e.g., "electronics" payment from a sanctioned country to an arms-
       embargoed destination)

=======================================================================
5. DECISION MATRIX FOR GEOGRAPHIC RISK
=======================================================================

| Scenario                               | Action                          |
|----------------------------------------|---------------------------------|
| Tier 1 country, amount < USD 10,000    | Standard processing             |
| Tier 1 country, amount >= USD 10,000   | CTR filing required             |
| Tier 2 country, any amount             | EDD required                    |
| Tier 3 country, amount < USD 1,000     | Analyst review required         |
| Tier 3 country, amount >= USD 1,000    | Senior officer approval needed  |
| Tier 4 (sanctioned) country, any       | BLOCK + regulatory report       |
| Sanctions list entity match            | BLOCK + immediate escalation    |
| PEP involvement, any geography         | Level 2 approval mandatory      |
| Structuring pattern detected           | STR filing + hold               |

=======================================================================
6. CORRESPONDENT BANKING CORRIDORS — RESTRICTED
=======================================================================

The following payment corridors have been restricted due to historical
abuse. Transactions on these routes require additional documentation:

  - India → UAE → Pakistan (common trade-based ML route)
  - Nigeria → China (wire fraud and invoice fraud risk)
  - Russia → Cyprus → EU (sanctions evasion risk)
  - Mexico → US (narco-trafficking proceeds risk)
  - Hong Kong → BVI (complex beneficial ownership)
  - Turkey → Iran (sanctions evasion via trade finance)

=======================================================================
7. DOCUMENTATION REQUIREMENTS
=======================================================================

For Tier 3 countries or amounts above USD 10,000, the following
documentation must be on file before payment is released:

  Mandatory:
    - Signed invoice or contract
    - Proof of relationship with beneficiary
    - Source of funds declaration
    - Ultimate Beneficial Owner (UBO) confirmation

  Recommended:
    - Bank statements for last 3 months
    - Business registration documents
    - Director/shareholder identification
